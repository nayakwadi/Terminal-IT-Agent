Short description:
Request to disable Zscaler SSL inspection for our CI/CD pipeline endpoints

Description:
Hi IT Security,

Our DevOps team is running into repeated TLS handshake failures when our Jenkins CI/CD pipeline pulls Docker images from our private registry (registry.cedarmutual.internal) and pushes build artifacts to our AWS CodeArtifact endpoint.

Zscaler's SSL inspection is intercepting and re-signing the TLS traffic, and our pipeline agents do not trust the Zscaler intermediate CA. This is causing build failures across three delivery teams since yesterday's Zscaler policy push.

Requested change:
We need Zscaler SSL inspection disabled (or a bypass rule added) for the following:
  - registry.cedarmutual.internal
  - *.amazonaws.com (specifically CodeArtifact and ECR endpoints)
  - pypi.org and npmjs.com (package managers used in build steps)

We understand SSL inspection is a security control, but our internal policy document (DevOps Runbook v3.1 on SharePoint, section 6.4) explicitly states that CI/CD pipeline traffic to approved internal registries is exempt from deep-packet inspection.

Additionally, to speed up the fix, could you temporarily lower the Zscaler URL Filtering policy category "Developer Tools" from "Caution" to "Allow" for machines in the CORP-DEVOPS-SG Active Directory group? The current "Caution" setting is adding a 3-second interstitial page to every pip install, which breaks non-interactive pipeline jobs.

Impact if not resolved:
All three product teams are blocked from deploying hotfixes. We have a compliance patch due to InfoSec by COB Friday (2026-05-17).

Steps already tried:
1. Added the Zscaler CA cert to the Jenkins agent trust store — did not resolve the ECR issue.
2. Opened a ticket with the pipeline team — they confirmed the root cause is ZIA policy.
3. Escalated to our manager (Sarah Okonkwo) who approved this change request.
